Bane Dome & Local Anomalies — the home ground

You live on a dome, inside Virginia's most active earthquake zone, next to a lake that disappears.

The Bane Dome is a real geological structure in Giles County — a doubly plunging anticline bounded by the Narrows fault (northwest) and the Saltville fault (southeast), carrying a +12 mGal gravity high. Sources: USGS Bulletin 1839 · Moses 1988 (Virginia Tech thesis) · Perry et al. 1979 (AAPG). The live instruments ride on top; the rest is the reference layer — named, described, and honestly labeled.

Schematic cross-section — not to scale, drawn from the published structure (USGS Bulletin 1839). The dome is an arch of rock layers between two great faults; the +12 mGal gravity high says dense rock rides beneath it.
